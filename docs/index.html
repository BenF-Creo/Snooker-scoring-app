Snooker Rules

Objective

Score more points than your opponent in a frame by potting balls in the correct order. A match is the best of an agreed number of frames.

Balls & values

  • Red — 1
  • Yellow — 2
  • Green — 3
  • Brown — 4
  • Blue — 5
  • Pink — 6
  • Black — 7

The standard game has 15 reds and the 6 colours; you can switch to the shorter 10-red game in Players & Setup.

Playing a frame

  1. While reds remain, you must pot a red first (1 point), then a colour of your choice. Reds stay down once potted; colours are returned to their spots.
  2. Keep alternating red, colour, red, colour… Your turn (your break) continues as long as you keep potting legally.
  3. Once all reds are gone, pot the final colour, then clear the six colours in ascending order: yellow, green, brown, blue, pink, black. These are not respotted.

The app dims any ball that isn’t legal to pot next, so just tap what went in.

Breaks

A break is the number of points scored in a single visit to the table. The app tracks your current break and your highest break of the frame.

Fouls

A foul ends your turn and awards penalty points to your opponent: a minimum of 4, or the value of the ball involved if higher (5 for blue, 6 for pink, 7 for black).

Winning

The player with more points once the black is potted wins the frame (a player who is well behind may concede). Win enough frames to take the match — the app shows “first to N”.

Billiards Rules

Objective

English Billiards is played with three balls: two cue balls (a plain white and a spot white — one per player) and a red object ball. You keep your own cue ball all game. First to the agreed target score wins.

Ways to score

  • Cannon — your cue ball strikes both other balls in one stroke: 2 points.
  • Pot red (winning hazard): 3 points.
  • In-off red (losing hazard — your cue ball into a pocket after hitting the red): 3 points.
  • Pot opponent’s white: 2 points.
  • In-off white: 2 points.

Several scores can be made in one stroke (e.g. a cannon that also goes in-off). Tap each scoring button as it happens — your break adds up.

Breaks & turns

You keep playing and accumulating points until you fail to score; then tap End Break and your opponent plays. The app tracks your current and highest break.

Fouls

In standard play a foul simply ends your turn and the stroke scores nothing — no points to your opponent. Just tap End Break. Use Undo to correct a mistaken tap.

Winning

The first player to reach the agreed target (often 100–300) wins. Set the target — or “No limit” — in Players & Setup; with no limit, tap Finish to end and compare scores.

Note: this is the casual scoring used by most clubs. Full tournament rules add limits (e.g. consecutive cannons, repeated pots/in-offs from baulk) that aren’t enforced here.
